# Application Vision: Helpdesk Ticketing System

## Background Story

TechCorp is a technology company with 500 employees. The IT support team handles dozens of issue requests every day through email and instant messaging. Management is chaotic, issues frequently slip through the cracks, and customers have no idea where their problem stands in the process.

The company decides to build a **Helpdesk Ticketing System** to bring order to everything.

---

## User Groups

| User Type | Who | Core Need |
|-----------|-----|-----------|
| **Customer** | TechCorp employee facing an IT issue | Submit issues, stay informed of progress in real time |
| **Agent** | IT support staff resolving issues | Efficiently manage all requests, never miss an issue |
| **Manager** | IT support supervisor | Understand team workload, ensure service quality |

---

## What We're Building

A three-role ticket management platform:

- Customers can submit tickets and track all their own issues
- Agents can see all tickets, take ownership, update, and resolve them
- Managers can view the overall picture and approve special requests

---
